Biography

About

Yuexuan (Joyce) Wang has performed as both a soloist and collaborative pianist across Asia, North America, and Europe. Her performances have taken place at distinguished venues, including the Shanghai Oriental Art Center in China, the National Music Hall of China in Beijing, Hongtai Music Hall in Xiamen, China, Teatro Comunale di Todi in Italy, Auer Hall at the Indiana University Jacobs School of Music, and the Concert Hall at Boston University. Wang has received top prizes in several competitions, including the Shanghai International Youth Piano Competition, the National Steinway Piano Competition (China), the Colorado International Piano Academy Competition, the Medici International Music Competition, and the London International Music Competition.

Wang is currently pursuing a Doctor of Musical Arts degree in Piano Performance at Boston University under the guidance of pianist Boaz Sharon, while also pursuing a certificate in Fundraising Management through Boston University's Metropolitan College. As a music educator, she is currently teaching group piano to both music majors and non-music majors, as well as aural skills at Boston University. She served as collaborative pianist for the Boston University Symphonic Chorus during the 2024–2025 season and for the children's choir at Old South Church in Boston during the 2025–2026 season.

Her previous studies include a Master of Music degree and a Performer Diploma from the Indiana University Jacobs School of Music, where she studied with distinguished pianists Arnaldo Cohen, a winner of the Ferruccio Busoni International Piano Competition; Émile Naoumoff, a protégé of Nadia Boulanger; and Edward Auer, a laureate of the International Chopin Piano Competition. She earned her bachelor's degree from the China Conservatory of Music in Beijing, where she studied with Singaporean pianist JingJing Lim. In addition to piano, Wang has studied harpsichord and fortepiano with Hsuan Chang Kitano, organ with Heinrich Christensen, and choral conducting with Gwyn Richards.

In addition to her formal studies, Wang has participated in numerous festivals and educational programs, including the Todi International Music Masters in Italy, the Virtuoso & Belcanto Festival in Lucca, Italy, the Gijón International Piano Festival in Spain, the Edward Auer Workshop in Indiana, and the Colorado International Piano Academy in the United States. Her musical development has been further enriched through masterclasses with distinguished pianists, including Antonio Pompa-Baldi, Stanislav Ioudenitch, Craig Sheppard, Ian Hobson, Till Fellner, and Jeffrey Cohen. In 2025, Wang also served as an assistant teacher at the Shanghai FYM International Music Festival.

Outside of music, Wang enjoys listening to podcasts and exploring vibe-coding, finding creativity and relaxation in virtual worlds as much as at the piano. She also developed a free web app, Practice Journal, designed to help musicians track their daily practice. She currently lives in Boston with her beloved cat, Frida, whose curious and independent spirit brings her daily joy and inspiration.
