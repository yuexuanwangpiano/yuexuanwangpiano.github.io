About

Yuexuan (Joyce) Wang

Wang has performed as a solo pianist across Asia, North America, and Europe at distinguished venues, including the Shanghai Oriental Art Center in China, the National Music Hall of China in Beijing, and Teatro Comunale di Todi in Italy.

A prizewinner of the National Steinway Piano Competition (China) and the Medici International Music Competition, Wang began studying piano at the age of five under the guidance of her mother, a soprano and choral conductor specializing in Chinese folk music. Wang is currently pursuing a Doctor of Musical Arts degree in Piano Performance at Boston University under the tutelage of pianist Boaz Sharon. As a music educator, she teaches group piano to both music majors and non-music majors, as well as aural skills to music students at Boston University.
